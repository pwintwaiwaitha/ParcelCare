ParcelCare | Autonomous Recovery Workflow

Mentor discussion brief · Updated proposal: AI recommends first, authorized humans opt in, then the
agent executes and follows up.

01 System workflow

                                                                  1 · Exception reported
                     Customer complaint OR rider failed-attempt report; tracking ID, details and optional photo evidence

                                                                2 · Create or match case
                  Use tracking ID plus issue context to merge duplicate reports; different issues can remain separate cases

                                                       3 · AI investigates and recommends
       Review shipment data, evidence, priority, permissions and available recovery actions; show proposed next step to office

                                                                                         staff

                                              4 · Staff chooses whether to activate AI recovery
                          Staff approves the suggested service/action; if declined, case stays on manual handling route

                                                      5 · AI executes within approved limits
                  Contacts customer or seller as applicable, uses validated backend tools, creates tasks and sends updates

                                                           6 · Physical work + verification
      Rider/operations complete assigned work; AI monitors statuses, escalates blockers and closes only after verified outcome

Key distinction

Office-staff approval activates the proposed AI service; it does not replace customer consent, seller authorization,
courier policy checks, or physical delivery confirmation.

02 What the AI does after staff approval

A · Wrong address -- courier-controlled recovery

                                                            AI proposes address recovery
                            Staff sees: "I can contact the customer, confirm a new address, and coordinate redelivery."

                                      Staff approves AI contacts customer
Customer supplies and confirms corrected address; AI checks delivery zone, parcel state and available slots

                                          AI updates and schedules
Only permitted changes are applied; backend creates a redelivery task and notifies customer/rider

                                                       Rider delivers AI verifies outcome
                                 Track task completion and delivery proof; unresolved or invalid requests go to staff

B · Wrong item / damaged parcel -- seller-dependent recovery

                                                           AI proposes seller coordination
                  Staff sees: "I can collect evidence and contact the registered seller for return/replacement authorization."

                      Staff approves AI contacts verified seller
For demo: teammate replies in an internal Seller Chat Simulator, not through real SMS

                                   AI checks seller decision and required permissions

Seller may approve return/replacement, request evidence, decline or defer; uncertain liability or unclear authority is
                                                                           escalated

                                               AI creates authorized operational tasks
Return pickup / replacement transport only when prerequisites are satisfied; rider handles physical collection and delivery

                                                 AI tracks outcome and escalates exceptions
                     Replacement "promised" is not "ready"; case closes only after required return/delivery confirmations

AI autonomy boundary

AI can investigate, message, interpret replies, plan and invoke controlled tools. It cannot independently approve
refunds, decide contested liability, invent seller consent, or mark an unfinished shipment as resolved.

04 Paid business-plan concept (proposal, not finalized)

Base plan             Manual exception management, dashboards, case visibility and status tracking.

AI Recovery plan      AI recommendations, staff-approved recovery execution, seller/customer follow-up
                      and audit trail.

Future advanced plan  Pre-approved policy automation for eligible low-risk cases, with spending/authority
                      limits and exception-only staff review.

Commercial and product considerations

Charge the logistics company, not the customer, for an optional AI recovery capability. Test whether customers
value reduced handling time and higher recovery completion enough to pay. Pricing is not yet validated. Requiring
staff approval for every case may reduce automation benefits; policy-based opt-in is a potential next step.
